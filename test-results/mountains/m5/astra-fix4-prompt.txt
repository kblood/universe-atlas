## Round 4 design direction (tune within it; your call on the mix, justify it)

The pit-free primitive is a strictly monotone function of height: h + f(h) with 1 + f'(h) > 0 has exactly the local
minima of h, on ANY surface, kinks included. So make STRATA BENCHES the primary rock texture and keep the downhill
gullies as the secondary one:
- bench profile sigma(t) on t = frac((carvedM + J)/P), sigma in [0, 1], sigma(0) = sigma(1) = 0, continuous, with a
  slow rise (the bench tread) and a fast return (the riser), and SMALL MEAN (the R1 leakage and toe troughs come
  from the mean). Monotone-height rule: A * max(sigma') / P < 1 with margin, where A is the bench amplitude (metres)
  and sigma' the derivative in t on the rising part. Example family: rise linearly-smooth over a tread fraction r >=
  A/(0.8 P), fall over a riser fraction, zero elsewhere; mean about (r + riser)/2. You may use 1 or 2 periods
  (e.g. 160 m and 70 m, incommensurate) to fill the 125-500 m octaves on 25-45 deg faces (horizontal period = P /
  slopeTan).
- J: a slowly varying jitter is fine only if you include its gradient in the bound; a seed-constant J is acceptable.
- gullies: keep the round-3 downhill kernels as a secondary term if they help R3'/R1e, with CONSTANT amplitude (no
  A_MIN) and smaller jitter (<= 0.3 cell) so the lattice stays near-regular (blue-noise-like, little power below the
  kernel frequency, which is what R4 needs), half-length well under 500 m.
- weight w(s) and the slope-limited depth stay; consider evaluating the weight with the same forward gradient but be
  explicit about its spatial derivative (it is the remaining path to a new pit at a toe).
- Cost <= 214 ns add-on in cost-bench.mjs (round 3: 157 ns unofficial).
- Update the HARD 6 inequality (your bound now covers benches + gullies), the L_MAX derivation, the digest, the
  render, the patch (git apply --check), and the integration script (rows R1e and R3' added; R1/R1d INFO).
