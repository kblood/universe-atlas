Left drag rotates. Right drag / Shift-drag pans. Scroll zooms. Touch: one finger rotates; two fingers pan and zoom.

Home fits the view. Backspace goes back. Breadcrumbs return to any parent level.

VR: Open this page in your headset browser and choose Enter VR. Point and pull a controller trigger to select, then use Explore in the floating panel. Left stick zooms and pans; right stick rotates the model. Hold a grip to move it. Back, Fit model and Exit VR are in the panel.

In a galaxy, select any mapped star and choose Approach selected star or Visit this system. Scroll toward stars to zoom freely. Fly freely enables drag-to-look, WASD movement, Q/E down/up, Shift boost, and wheel speed adjustment. Escape stops flight. Flight works between galaxies, stars and planets; speed adapts automatically. Fly to selection steers to a chosen object, and Fly outward leaves a system or galaxy. On touch screens, hold Fly forward and drag the scene to steer.

Positions are physical. Small bodies use enlarged markers so they remain selectable. Galaxy map points are real catalogue systems. Nearby stars stream in around you while the larger galaxy stays in place.

Planets: Approach low orbit flies to 30–100 km altitude. Descend to 10 km stops higher over mountains. Hold orbit circles the planet smoothly through the poles; drag to look and use movement keys to release it. The wheel changes orbit speed. Free flight keeps a 120 m terrain-clearance floor. Land and walk prepares a solid surface: WASD walks, Shift runs, Space jumps. Return to flight takes off. Walking uses desktop controls; oceans remain closed. Surface destinations surveys named highlands, plains, coasts and snow sites. Choose a site, then land on arrival. Ground detail toggles the close sand, soil, rock and snow materials. Atmospheric horizon assist gently levels free-flight roll toward the planet while you control pitch and heading; switch it off to keep a bank. Inland rivers and their regional lakes are currently unavailable; older river links open the ordinary world. Walking stops at the ocean; tree and stone collision is still pending. Fly over terrain chooses land and descends to a 600 m terrain-following flight. Select a star and align it with the reticle to fly there with W.

Universal date: seek in years since the Big Bang. Stars, planets, surfaces and nearby life follow their modeled dates. The earliest universe and far future remain coarse placeholders. Walking locations and exact dates are saved in the URL.
