# Revised surface lighting and detailed-family follow-up

These are local desktop captures from the Atlas Vite app at `http://127.0.0.1:5197/`, on an RTX 5090 using ANGLE/D3D11. The reports preserve exact runtime and asset hashes. The captured lighting runtime corresponds to `898a87f`; later source changes were not part of these captures. No XR/headset validation was performed.

## Runs

- [`revised/failure.json`](../revised/failure.json) records the first attempt. It stopped when `lifePackBuilds` was still changing; `faunaTerrainPending` was nonzero in the detailed phase. Treat this as an incomplete warm-up, not a demonstrated runtime regression. Its [failure screenshot](../revised/failure-page.png) and [legacy detailed](../revised/legacy-detailed-close.png) / [legacy basic](../revised/legacy-basic-close.png) canvas captures are preserved.
- [`revised-run-2/report.json`](../revised-run-2/report.json) completed legacy and family comparisons, but the harness left surface lighting in basic mode for the family captures. Those family images therefore do not test detailed-lit family rendering.
- [`report.json`](report.json) is the corrected detailed-lit follow-up. It waits for both `lifePending` and `faunaTerrainPending` to drain. Across six 500 ms samples, legacy `lifePackBuilds` stayed at 362; `patchLookupCache` stayed at 16,545 hits, 5,627 misses, and 5,627 keys. Both queues were zero.

The corrected run loaded the same Oak family with autogenerated mips and with the coverage-preserving mip option at the same close pose/date. Each had 30 near and 306 mid trees. The cold loads took 2,742 ms and 2,747 ms; the coverage-mip setup took 322 ms and produced 12 levels. The mip chains target base foliage coverage 0.1897. At the captured pose, the images differed in 2.60% of pixels by more than 3 channel values, with mean maximum-channel delta 0.361. The two images looked nearly alike; this does not establish a perceptual improvement.

At the close pose, detailed lighting was active at 11.75 m clearance. At the mid capture it remained active at 387.1 m, with shadow intensity about 0.878. The close and mid images are [legacy detailed](legacy-detailed-lit-close.png), [family default mips](family-default-mips-detailed-lit-close.png), [family coverage mips](family-coverage-mips-detailed-lit-close.png), and [mid view](family-coverage-mips-detailed-lit-mid.png). Browser errors and GL errors were zero.

The 8-second detailed-lit samples report CPU `planetUpdateMs` p50/p95 of 34.3/43.2 ms for legacy, 34.1/42.0 ms for default-mip family, and 34.7/44.6 ms for coverage-mip family. Instrumented GPU pass-sum p50/p95 were 11.14/15.47, 9.84/18.33, and 13.07/20.61 ms. These are observations from different capture windows, not controlled performance claims; there is no separate shadow-pass timing. Family draws/triangles were 291 / 10,255,260 versus legacy 283 / 1,259,568.

## Date-probe limitation

Find Life changed the in-game date from the URL's requested `435084631200000032044300000` to `435084631200000074296400000`. The wind/date probe incorrectly based its shift and restore on the requested URL date. Consequently `datePhaseMatches` is false, and the wind-shift image and subsequent mid capture are not valid evidence for date-driven wind behavior. The captured `windPhase01` values are per-instance offsets, not the shared animation phase. Future runs must record `window.__atlas.date.offsetNs` after Find Life and derive every date change from that observed value.

The screenshots still show hard, high-contrast shadows, dark foliage undersides, and faceted crowns. Detailed-family appearance and wind behavior are not approved by these checks.

## Reproduction

With the local Vite server running and the pinned family files still under `Assets/_assetctl/atlas-ez-family-f`:

```powershell
$env:ATLAS_HARDWARE='1'
$env:ATLAS_RESULTS='test-results/surface-lighting/revised-family-lighting-replay'
node test-results/surface-lighting/revised-family.browser.mjs
```

The earlier harnesses and full diagnostics are kept with each run.
