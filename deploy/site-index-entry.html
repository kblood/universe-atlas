✧ Universe Atlas

Explore a seeded 3D universe on desktop or in VR. Zoom from galaxies to named stars, fly between systems and planetary orbits, and land on solid planets and moons. Walk, run and jump over streamed terrain, follow named river banks, and explore lakeshores with reflected water, grass, ferns, stones and textured trees. Continents and oceans retain their geography as you approach, while the surrounding universe stays in view. A star map, bookmarks, shared location links and atmospheric horizon assistance make travel easier. Inspect generated plants, animal specimens, habitat ranges and evolutionary lineages in the Biosphere Lab, alongside the original biome reference plots. Seek a universal date to update orbital positions; cosmic evolution and planet-wide ecosystems remain in development.
