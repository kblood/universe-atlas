## Live checks

- The guide audit checked all 9 chapters at 1440, 390 and 320 pixel widths;
  all 74 internal destinations resolved and there were no reported errors.
- In-game guide link and era-page checks completed without errors.
- Luna's RTX 5090 flight/walking fixture traversed 320.7 m in flight and
  323.1 m on foot. It finished with 153,514 trees in 6,688 patches, no census
  mismatches, queued patches, worker failures, capacity misses, or browser
  errors. Framebuffer alpha was fully opaque at MSAA 0, 2 and 4.
- The live date replay moved water from −4,500 m to −4,475 m and back. All

  RTX 5090 at 1440×1000. CPU frame-work p95 was 22.6 ms, CPU planet-update p95
  16.9 ms, and GPU pass-sum p95 6.77 ms. These are diagnostic profile results,
  not an overall frame-rate or 60 FPS claim. Cloud scene-sampling captures at
  MSAA 0, 2 and 4 are included; this record does not declare the antialiasing
  design final.

## Included rendering changes and limits

The release contains canopy runtime through `98556a7`: full canonical forest
patches, consistent near/far ownership, altitude-based prefetch and rendering,
bounded instance updates, water-validity reuse, and retained terrain refresh
when date changes alter water level. The water review follow-ups are included
in this release. See the [water interval design](canopy-water-validity-design-2026-09-28.md),
[Opus water review](opus-5.5-canopy-water-review-2026-09-28.md),
[review assessment](canopy-water-review-assessment-2026-09-28.md), and
[terrain date-refresh record](terrain-water-refresh-2026-09-28.md).

Rendered trees still use Atlas's legacy faceted proxy shapes. No realistic
authored tree assets or species-geometry integration shipped in this increment.
The tests used RTX 5090 hardware; they do not establish performance on a
representative midrange PC, a general frame-rate guarantee, or XR acceptance.
