`f3f18a8dcc068b04d4ce7514c0882b5643b7be3f`, consuming creature-kit 0.5.0 from
`4a5630fab9b0027ea1825fae18f5afb2cb305928`. The independent canonical Animal Lab


close animals at 1,500 triangles and 112 middle animals at 750 through 200 m.

from the additional 32 MiB cold typed reserve and unknown worker JS heap.

landmark pins, packed coefficients, sparse uploads and specialized materials
