## Coordinator diagnosis of round 2 (Opus, coordinator; overrulable by the owner)

Round 2 met cost (151 ns add-on, unofficial) and runtime (1.25 s), but two measurements show ONE design flaw:

1. R1d fails, 0.75 (gate), because the flat class's band-pass RMS of the detail field is 17.5 m although rock is
   exactly 0 on every flat input pixel. That RMS is footprint leakage of the rock's MEAN: shape has a large mean
   (ribs (1-|.8g|)^2 ~ 0.6, strata (t-t^3)/.385 mean 0.65), so every steep face is lowered by roughly
   0.6 x 120 = 70 m as a block, and the 500 m box at a floor pixel next to a wall sees that step.
2. The real-M3 integration smoke (seed 730201, one site; integration-m3-smoke.json) shows the consequence for
   drainage: with M5 the M3 row pits025Per1000km2P50 goes 2.6 -> 54 (FAILS <= 50) and all strict pits 134.7 -> 1914.5
   per 1000 km2; slope p90 46.9 -> 48.7 (above the 48.4 guard). Isotropic ribs of up to 96 m depth on 125-250 m cells
   cut contour-parallel troughs into the faces, and the block-lowered face toes sit below the unlowered floors: closed
   depressions. The fixture R5 did not see it because the fixture's analytic faces are planar and its floors drain
   along y.

The plan's own rule decides the fix: M5 must not break any M3 row (the drainage rows are the calibrated M3 rows).

## Round 3 design (implement this; tune only within it)

Make the relief drain by construction: texture that is elongated ALONG THE FALL LINE (gullies/couloirs and ribs
running downhill) plus the existing height-monotone strata, with a depth that scales with the local slope so that,
going downhill, the detail can never climb faster than the terrain falls.

API change (the module has never been merged; version string stays 'mountain-rock-detail-1'):
- `rockDetailM(alongM, acrossM, carvedM, carvedXM, carvedYM, seed)`: carvedXM = carved height (M2+M3, no rock) at
  (alongM + SLOPE_STEP_M, acrossM), carvedYM at (alongM, acrossM + SLOPE_STEP_M). The module computes the forward
  gradient gx = (carvedXM - carvedM)/S, gy = (carvedYM - carvedM)/S, slopeTan = sqrt(gx^2 + gy^2), and the downhill
  unit vector e = -(gx, gy)/slopeTan (only after the weight exclusion, so slopeTan > tan 10 deg and the division is
  safe). Non-finite carvedXM/carvedYM -> literal +0 too.
- Keep `carvedSlopeTan(hC, hX, hY)` and `rockSlopeWeight` exported (tests and the patch use them).
- Update the wiring patch (mountainRidgeTermM passes U + C, U + Cx, U + Cy) and re-check `git apply --check`.

Field:
- weight w = rockSlopeWeight(slopeTan) as now (exact 0 at/below tan 10, 1 at/above tan 30).
- depth = min(MAX_DEPTH_M, CAP_FRACTION * carvedM, KAPPA * slopeTan * GULLY_HALF_LENGTH_M). The third term is the
  pit guard: along the fall line the gully profile's derivative is at most B / GULLY_HALF_LENGTH_M (B = the kernel
  profile's maximum slope in its normalised coordinate, e.g. 1.5396 for (1-z^2)^2), so the detail's downhill climb rate
  is <= KAPPA * B * slopeTan * RIB_WEIGHT; choose KAPPA so that KAPPA * B * RIB_WEIGHT + (strata term, below) <= 0.6.
  State the derivation in a comment and assert the inequality on the frozen constants (HARD 6).
- gullies: sparse-convolution kernels on a jittered square lattice of cell GULLY_CELL_M (e.g. 250 m): for each of the
  3 x 3 cells around the sample, one hash gives a centre jitter (in [0.15, 0.85] of the cell, both axes) and an
  amplitude a in [A_MIN, 1]. With d = sample - centre, du = d . e (downhill), dw = d . n (n = e rotated 90 deg), the
  kernel is K = a * P(du / GULLY_HALF_LENGTH_M) * P(dw / GULLY_HALF_WIDTH_M) with P(z) = (1 - z^2)^2 for |z| < 1 and 0
  otherwise (C1, compact). gully = max over the 9 kernels (continuous; the max keeps the derivative bound of a single
  kernel). Suggested starting point: half-length 400 m, half-width 60-90 m (so the cross-section sits at 125-350 m
  wavelengths and the length below 1 km). The kernels are oriented by e at the SAMPLE (not at the centre), so the
  lever arm |d| is at most ~2 cells and the field stays continuous where e varies continuously.
  Sparse grooves keep the mean small (target: mean(gully) <= 0.25 on a uniform steep plane; report it), which is
  what removes the R1d leakage and the toe troughs.
- strata: keep `t = frac((carvedM + J)/P)` and (t - t^3)/0.3849..., monotone in height as proved (STRATA_WEIGHT *
  depth <= 0.3849 * P). J must stay cheap; a jitter derived from the gully kernels' hashes or one gradient-noise octave
  is fine within the cost budget. Its contribution to the downhill climb rate is <= STRATA_WEIGHT * depth * 2.598 / P
  * slopeTan... (derive the correct bound, including J's gradient, and include it in the 0.6 inequality).
- shape = clamp01(STRATA_WEIGHT * strata + RIB_WEIGHT * gully), v = -w * depth * shape, as now.
- Delete the isotropic ribs function (keep a copy of the round-2 shape ONLY as a variant source string inside the test
  for the negative controls below).

Cost: the add-on (2 offset M3 lookups + rockDetailM) must stay <= 214 ns in cost-bench.mjs (round 2: 151 ns). Nine
kernels with one hash each, early exit before any kernel when w = 0, and skipping kernels whose |du| >= half-length or
|dw| >= half-width before evaluating P are expected to fit. Measure it.
