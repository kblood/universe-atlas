### Module contract: `src/mountain-rock-detail.ts`

Arithmetic only, same audit as M3: `+ - * /`, `Math.floor/sqrt/imul/min/max/abs`, comparisons, integer hashing. No
other Math function, no `**`, no Number/parseFloat/Date, no imports at all (the module is self-contained). Every value
can feed a height and the ocean flag, so it must be bit-reproducible across engines.

Exports (names fixed):

- `MOUNTAIN_ROCK_DETAIL_VERSION = 'mountain-rock-detail-1'`.
- `MOUNTAIN_ROCK_DETAIL`: frozen constants object. It must contain at least:
  - `SLOPE_ZERO_TAN: 0.17632698070846498` (tan 10 deg; written as this literal, the plan's "below 10 deg" class) and
    `SLOPE_FULL_TAN: 0.5773502691896257` (tan 30 deg, the plan's "above 30 deg" class);
  - `SLOPE_STEP_M: 125` (the fixed metric step of the slope estimate; the plan's "slope at 125 m");
  - `MAX_DEPTH_M` (the absolute amplitude bound, metres; it must be <= 200) and `CAP_FRACTION: 0.5`;
  - the octave / strata constants you choose (wavelengths, gains, strata periods). Tuning is allowed ONLY on the
    tuning seeds (below).
- `ROCK_DETAIL_BANDS`: frozen array of frozen `{octave, wavelengthM}` naming the declared bands for M0/LOD, like
  `CARVING_BANDS`. Longest declared wavelength = 1000 m (the plan: "wavelengths under 1 km").
- `ROCK_DETAIL_MAX_EDGE_M`: the largest tile edge that can hold the band, by the SAME rule lod-ladder.ts uses for the
  carving: `longestWavelengthM / 2 * 32 / 2` = 8000 m (lattice Nyquist 2*edge/32 <= half the longest band). Export the
  inputs too (`ROCK_DETAIL_REDUCTION = {longestWavelengthM: 1000, latticeSegments: 32}`), frozen. This module does not
  import lod-ladder.ts; the T6 patch adds the level helper there.
- `rockSlopeWeight(slopeTan)`: exactly +0 when `!(slopeTan > SLOPE_ZERO_TAN)` (NaN included); exactly 1 when
  `slopeTan >= SLOPE_FULL_TAN`; a C1 smoothstep in between (t*t*(3-2t)). Continuous.
- `carvedSlopeTan(hC, hX, hY)`: the slope (tan) of a height field from forward differences at the fixed step
  `SLOPE_STEP_M`: `sqrt(((hX-hC)/S)^2 + ((hY-hC)/S)^2)` with hX = h(along+S, across), hY = h(along, across+S). The
  caller evaluates the CARVED surface (M3 output, without rock) at those three points with a fixed metric step that
  never depends on a tile level or lattice spacing; a forward difference of a continuous field at a fixed step is
  continuous, which is what keeps tile edges free of cracks/speckle.
- `rockDetailSeed(carvingSeed)`: a 32-bit integer hash of the M3 strip seed with a fixed salt (so rock and carving
  noise are decorrelated); `>>> 0`.
- `rockDetailM(alongM, acrossM, carvedM, slopeTan, seed)`: the detail in metres. Contract:
  - returns literal `+0` (Object.is(v, 0)) when `rockSlopeWeight(slopeTan) === 0`, when `!(carvedM > 0)`, or when
    alongM / acrossM is non-finite (use the M3 idioms: `x - x !== 0`, negated comparisons). Never returns -0 or NaN.
  - otherwise `-weight * depth * shape` with `depth = min(MAX_DEPTH_M, CAP_FRACTION * carvedM)` and
    `shape` in [0, 1]. Hence for every input: `-min(MAX_DEPTH_M, CAP_FRACTION*carvedM) <= v <= 0`, so
    `carvedM + v >= (1 - CAP_FRACTION) * carvedM >= 0` (coast/floor safety: the relief floor's F >= 0 where a0 >= 0
    keeps holding when the T6 patch adds v to the mountain term) and `carvedM + v <= carvedM` (UPPER ENVELOPE: every
    coarser level that drops the rock detail is >= the truth, as contract section 3 requires of a declared
    reduction; this is why v must never be positive).
  - `carvedM` is the carved mountain term U + C (M2 envelope plus M3 carving, pre-radius-scale metres) at the sample;
    it is both the cap and the height the strata read.
  - shape = a fixed blend of two parts, both deterministic and continuous in (alongM, acrossM, carvedM):
    (a) STRATA (strata-aligned ledges): a terrace function of the height, `t = frac((carvedM + J)/P)` with a smooth
        gradient-noise phase jitter J(along, across) (so ledges wander and dip instead of being perfect contours),
        and `strata(t) = (t - t*t*t) / 0.3849001794597505` (in [0,1], zero at t = 0 and t = 1, so continuous across
        the wrap; the kink at the wrap is the ledge edge). Use 1 or 2 strata periods P (vertical metres). Keep the
        strata amplitude per period <= 0.3849*P so the terraced profile stays monotone in height (no reversed slope,
        hence no closed depression along a ledge).
    (b) RIDGED rock ribs/gullies: ridged 2D gradient noise (Perlin-style gradients from a fixed table of directions,
        quintic or cubic fade, no trig) in (alongM, acrossM), 2-4 octaves with cell sizes chosen so the energy sits
        at 125 m - 1 km wavelengths, shape `(1 - |g|)^2`-style, normalised into [0,1].
    Combine as `shape = clamp01(wS*strata + wR*ribs)` with wS + wR = 1 (constants frozen).
  - Gradient noise, not value noise (value noise leaks energy to long wavelengths; the band-limit row below checks).
  - Coordinates can be up to |along| = 2e7 m: compute lattice indices with Math.floor on along/cell and keep the
    fractional part well conditioned (subtract the floor), hash the integer cell with Math.imul like M3's `hash`.
