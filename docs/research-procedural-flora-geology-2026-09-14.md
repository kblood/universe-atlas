# Procedural flora and geology: a hierarchical, inspectable proposal

## Current direction — 2026-09-27

The [desktop realism plan](desktop-realism-implementation-plan-2026-09-27.md) adopts the species-to-individual hierarchy for the next implementation. Species rules, bounded structural mesh variants and per-instance bark/leaf colours and controlled shape changes are separate responsibilities. This dated research remains context, not an implementation report.

_Research note, 2026-09-14. This report changes no runtime code, assets, dependencies, or baselines._

## Decision

Replace the present fixed recipe palette gradually with **hierarchical generators**. A placement must name a clade, species, genotype/variant, developmental stage and environmental response before it names a mesh. The generator then selects a reusable, baked prototype and an instance variation from those stable inputs. This gives a pine stand a shared identity, lets its individuals differ for intelligible reasons, and avoids treating a random scale/rotation as biological variety.

The existing `biome-ecology.ts` is a good deterministic placement-fixture boundary: it has global-addressed candidates, a competition halo, stable IDs, separate grass coexistence and recipe-level counts. It deliberately carries only `recipeId`, size, rotation and colour. Its 19 records (for example `oak`, `pine`, `rounded-rock`, `slab-rock`) are therefore useful v1 representatives, but not a morphology contract. `biome-lab-spec.md` is equally clear that its distinct forms and coverage estimates are a controlled representation test, not a realism certificate. The earlier vegetation/rocks audit records the corresponding runtime limitation: one tree architecture, one fern and one shared deformed stone geometry cannot demonstrate broad biological or geological diversity. These constraints should remain visible in reports while the data model grows.

The target is not a whole-planet growth simulation. It is a repeatable asset-and-placement system in which a species has a recognizable structural signature across many cells, a nearby individual can be old, wind-pruned or drought-stressed without becoming a different species, and rock shape follows origin, fabric and exposure rather than an unrelated noise seed.

## Plant identity: from clade to individual

Use four immutable, versioned records, plus a derived prototype cache.

| Level | Stable inputs and meaning | Output / allowable variation |
|---|---|---|
| Clade / architectural family | branching mode, phyllotaxis, axis persistence, leaf class, root/anchor class, material family | broadleaf monopodial tree, conifer, graminoid, cushion shrub, reed, fictional analogue |
| Species | trait distributions and correlations, habitat envelope, grammar/solver program version, seasonal palette, `body + speciesSeed` derivation | a recognizable generated species and its bounded prototype set, reusable in every compatible biome |
| Individual | `planet + ecologyVersion + species + cell + candidate` key, inherited latent values, age and damage history | deterministic prototype choice, proportions, pose, material variation, optional animation phase |
| Environmental expression | local wind, light/open-canopy, soil/substrate, wetness/salinity, slope, disturbance and season | bounded plastic response: crown asymmetry, leaf density, height cap, lean, colour and dieback |

A clade/family is authored as a constrained grammar and trait prior; it must not be the entire fixed species catalogue. Derive multiple species parameter records from `(bodySeed, ecologyVersion, familyId, speciesSeed)` subject to that family’s constraints, then allow a generated species to recur in all compatible biomes on that body. A species record should contain distributions for height, crown ratio, taper, branching-angle/rhythm, internode length, leaf size/density, root/rock attachment and phenology, rather than independent min/max sliders. Draw a small latent vector once per individual and derive correlated traits from it: a tall, fast-growing individual can also have a relatively narrow young crown and longer internodes; an exposed species can have a shorter, denser crown and stronger lean. Encode the correlation matrix, distribution parameters and sampling algorithm in the record/version. It prevents implausible combinations and makes an individual reproducible.

Growth stage must be an explicit ordinal or normalized age, not merely `sizeM`: seedling, juvenile, mature, senescent and dead/snags need their own topology/prototype eligibility. Environmental plasticity is an authored response model, not evidence that the application predicts botany. For an alien plant, retain the same schema but mark every invented clade, trait range and response curve `speculative`; do not cite Earth plant papers as validation of it. This separation permits coherent alien morphology without presenting it as science.

L-systems are a compact language for recursively specified plant development and have been extended to cover plant geometry and development; they are well suited to a deterministic authored species grammar library ([Prusinkiewicz et al., 2018](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/modeling-plant-development-with-l-systems.html)). Parametric and stochastic productions make dimensions, probabilities and stage parameters first-class, which is a better fit for clade/species records than a palette of primitive shapes. Their limitation here is practical: naïvely expanding a detailed grammar for every visible plant makes cost and topology grow with scene population, and an L-system alone does not decide how a crown avoids a neighbouring canopy.

Space colonization starts with attraction points and grows branch tips toward them; Runions et al. show it can model leaf/vein structures and trees ([Runions, Lane and Prusinkiewicz, 2007](https://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/colonization.egwnp2007.pdf)). It is valuable for a small number of near/hero crown skeletons because the attractor field directly controls envelope, gaps and obstacle avoidance. It is less attractive as the only species definition: its envelope and sampling parameters can dominate the recognizable architecture, and repeated online solves are poor streaming work.

Structural/functional models combine branching rules with resource, light and signalling constraints. Pałubicki et al. demonstrate self-organizing tree and shrub forms with local competition ([Pałubicki et al., 2009](https://research.adobe.com/publication/self-organizing-tree-models-for-image-synthesis/)). This is scientific support for a **bounded offline or worker-side prototype bake**, not for simulating every tree every frame. The recommended hybrid is parametric structural grammar for species identity; optional space-colonization or bounded self-organization only while producing a finite set of mature prototype skeletons; then bake clean meshes, cards/impostors and collision representations. All runtime plants select those finite outputs.

Grass, reeds, shrubs and ground plants need no forced miniature-tree grammar. Give each a family-specific generator: blades from a clump centre with correlated length/arc/tilt; reeds from a stem bundle and wetness-driven height; cushion shrubs from radial modules and wind-facing dieback. A grass placement remains a patch, as the lab already specifies, and must not be reported as an individual organism.

## Geology: describe a rock’s history before its noise

The equivalent hierarchy for rocks is `lithology → formation/transport context → exposure/weathering state → individual`. A rock recipe needs at least:

* lithology/fabric: massive crystalline, vesicular lava, bedded sedimentary, foliated metamorphic, unconsolidated regolith;
* origin/context: in-place outcrop, talus, fluvial transport, glacial transport, beach, impact ejecta or volcanic deposit;
* fracture set(s): dominant orientation, spacing, persistence, aperture and block aspect; and
* weathering agents and exposure: freeze–thaw, salt, water abrasion, chemical dissolution, wind abrasion, biological cover, burial and time proxy.

These attributes determine silhouette and material together. Bedded sediment tends toward slabs with aligned strata and preferential planar breakage; talus tends toward angular, source-matched fragments and downhill alignment; sufficiently transported fluvial or beach material tends toward rounding, sorting and abrasion. These are conditional tendencies, not universal classifiers: short transport, resistant lithology, fresh breakage and later disturbance can retain angular clasts. Massive volcanic rocks can be blocky or vesicular; regolith can be fragments and fines rather than uniformly scaled boulders. Colour, roughness, mineral grains, lichen and wetness masks must be conditioned by the same history. Never let a smooth random blob acquire a sedimentary material label merely by colour.

Graphics research supports process-informed approximations while also setting their limit. Dorsey, Edelman, Jensen, Legakis and Pedersen model weathered stone through water transport, dissolution and surface erosion, but explicitly call their result phenomenological rather than an exact physical prediction ([Dorsey et al., 1999](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/graphics/pubs/stone.pdf)). Mateos, Carranza, Susín and Argudo use fracture structure and an invasion-percolation approach for mechanical weathering and shard formation ([Mateos et al., 2024](https://diglib.eg.org/bitstream/handle/10.2312/ceig20241139/01_ceig20241139.pdf)). For v1, use these as evidence for selecting coherent fracture/weathering controls, then bake a limited prototype family; do not claim the game’s simplified rules model real geological time, mechanics or mineral chemistry.

## Browser and Quest delivery

Generate expensive geometry away from the render loop and keep a per-species/rock cache keyed by generator version, prototype seed, LOD and material variant. Set an explicit per-species prototype budget by platform and LOD (including a cap on concurrently resident generated species); variation comes from both the bounded prototype set and cheap instance traits, never an unbounded stream of unique meshes. A streamed cell stores only stable placement traits and references; it should never retain every expanded branch graph. Share one geometry/material per `(speciesPrototype, lod, materialFamily)` and place transforms plus compact per-instance attributes (tint, age expression, wind phase/strength, damage mask). Three.js `InstancedMesh` is expressly for many objects sharing geometry and material and reduces draw calls ([Three.js, *InstancedMesh*](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html)). Update its instance bounds after changing transforms and dispose retired instance resources, as its documentation requires.

Use three representations, selected per platform budget and projected size: near opaque mesh with simplified leaves/needles and an optional independent deforming foliage rig; middle clump/card or very low-poly mesh; far impostor/coverage field, then no individual object. Keep trunks, foliage, roots/collision and wind rigs independently optional. A static shrub or rock has no rig requirement; a wind-animated plant must have a tested deformation representation, which should not affect collision, identity or topology validation. Do not make transparent blended crowns the default on Quest; favour masked/opaque representations and measure the actual device.

Cell streaming must be deterministic and bounded: derive placements from global coordinates, request a halo calculated from the maximum active placement influence radius (canopy, root/collision and competition rule) plus the required candidate-cell margin, and version it with those rules. Do not reuse the lab’s current 16 m halo as a universal ecological constant. Cache decoded prototypes with an LRU byte budget, and evict only reconstructible cell instance buffers. Throttle generation/asset decode and prefetch on camera velocity, while preserving a visible fallback LOD. Group batches by prototype/material and cell so culling removes whole batches. Record CPU typed-array bytes, resident geometry/texture counts, shader/program count, draw calls and triangles. Three.js exposes active geometry/texture counts and per-frame calls/triangles through `WebGLRenderer.info` ([Three.js, *WebGLRenderer*](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/WebGLRenderer.html)); those are useful engineering counters, not a universal Quest performance guarantee. Profile frame time, thermals and memory in the intended headset/browser before setting acceptance caps.

glTF/GLB is preferred for exported baked prototypes, with metres, Y-up/right-handed transforms, UVs, deterministic names, applied pivots and LOD/collision siblings. Runtime-generated Three.js `BufferGeometry` and instance buffers need not be serialized as GLBs; they still require deterministic provenance records and disposal. Package the brief, source/prototype seeds, generator version, source consent/licence and manifest beside every export/bake. A research paper may be read and cited; it does not grant a code or asset licence. Code licensing, asset/export ownership, source-image consent and model/checkpoint terms are separate checks. In particular, Proc-Rock is GPL-3.0 ([repository and licence declaration](https://github.com/acfaruk/proc-rock)); do not copy or embed its code without an explicit licence decision, even though the repository says exported rocks are the creator's. The same provenance requirement applies to scanned textures, reference imagery, model checkpoints and generated outputs.

## V1 increment and acceptance nursery

V1 should replace no entire planetary system. Author three plant-family priors (broadleaf, conifer, graminoid/reed), two shrub-family priors and four rock histories (bedded slab/outcrop, talus fragment, fluvial cobble, volcanic clinker). From each family, generate multiple species trait records using body/species seeds rather than shipping only five fixed Earth recipes; provide a small deterministic fixture bank of generated species for review. Every generated species receives a platform-capped 3–6 structural prototypes across its near/mid/far representations, a correlated-trait schema and a provenance manifest. Keep existing ecological placement rules, mapping recipe IDs to compatible families/species/history fixtures; add `speciesId`, `prototypeId`, `lifeStage`, `traitSeed`, `expression` and `geologyId` to the export/diagnostic model. Earth analogue names should be labelled visual/architectural references unless the numeric traits have a stated source.

The nursery is a deterministic review suite, not a benchmark that can prove realism:

| Fixture | Required observations / machine checks |
|---|---|
| Same-species grove | fixed seed produces identical IDs, traits and prototype selection; 24 mature individuals share branching/leaf signature while differing in bounded height, crown, lean and tint; normalized, uncoloured silhouette/shape descriptors are clustered for review, not accepted as proof of identity |
| Same-family species row | at least three generated species from one family differ in declared correlated traits and uncoloured normalized silhouette/shape descriptors, while each remains internally recognizable; a broadleaf-versus-conifer comparison alone is insufficient |
| Cross-family transect | broadleaf, conifer, shrub and grass remain recognizably distinct in uncoloured silhouette and normalized shape descriptors at near and middle LODs; no identity is inferred solely from colour, hashes or recolours |
| Stage and plasticity strip | juvenile/mature/senescent plus sheltered/windy/dry/wet expressions preserve species identity and remain within declared trait bounds |
| Rock provenance bed | slabs show coherent bedding/fracture direction, talus is angular and slope-associated, cobbles are rounded and water-associated, volcanic prototypes retain their fabric/material pairing |
| Asset technical sheet | finite/nondegenerate geometry, normals/UVs, applied transforms/pivots, material/texture limits, collision and LOD presence as applicable; rig independence is checked only for animated flora |
| Streaming flight | revisiting and overlapping cell requests yields identical instances; bounded cache/draw/triangle/resource counters; repeated load/evict cycles dispose buffers without growth |
| Headset review | intended Quest browser/device reports frame time, visible popping, aliasing/overdraw and memory/thermal observations at defined routes and quality settings |

Capture front, side and canopy/ground silhouettes, topology and material sheets, and same-seed/different-seed comparisons. Human art/technical review decides whether a species reads coherently and whether variety is sufficient. Counts, coverage, draw calls, image similarity or any other image metric can reveal regressions, but none proves biological, geological or visual realism.
